# Frontline

Army mod for NeoForge 1.21.1. Blocky, realistically proportioned firearms with first-person and over-the-shoulder
shooting, villager recruiting, Command Flag bases, and named nations with their own armies.

| UC-45 Submachine Gun | .45 | 25 | auto, 600 rpm | heavy rounds, folding stock |
| BR-20 Marksman Rifle | 7.62 | 20 | semi | 2.5x scope (scroll to zoom) |
| GL-40 Grenade Launcher | 40mm | 6 | revolving | explosive grenades that arc |

- **Left mouse** fires (hold for automatics). **Right mouse** aims down the sights (FOV zoom, tighter spread;
  sneak + right-click still interacts with blocks). **R** reloads from ammo in your inventory. **V** toggles the
  over-the-shoulder camera; the crosshair stays true because bullets are fired toward what the camera ray hits.
- Scoped rifles: the scroll wheel changes magnification (1.5x–10x) while you look through the scope, and mouse sensitivity drops with zoom.
- Recoil kicks the camera and the gun; spread blooms with sustained fire and shrinks when crouched or aimed.
- Bullets are tracer entities with light drop after one second. Headshots deal double damage. Hit markers and
  kill markers sound on the shooter's client.
- Every gun is a JSON element model (28 to 54 cuboids each) textured from one material atlas, built by
  `gen/gen_guns.py`; sounds are synthesised by `gen/gen_sounds.py`.

## Armor and goggles

Combat Helmet, Plate Carrier, Combat Pants and Combat Boots use the vanilla armor layers, so they sit just outside
the body and never clip through the player or the guns. A full set gives 17 armor points and cuts bullet damage by
32% (the plate carrier alone 16%). The **NVG Helmet** (combat helmet + 2 glass panes + redstone + copper) has
goggles: press **N** to cycle night vision → thermal → off.
- Night vision: green phosphor image with grain, scanlines and a binocular mask; the dark becomes readable.
- Thermal: terrain goes cold purple, living things glow yellow-white (undead read cold), crewed vehicles run warm.

### Camo variants, recruits' uniforms

Every armor piece comes in **Woodland**, **Arctic** (craft with white dye) and **Black Ops** (black dye). Recruits are
issued a full set in their army's camo (`/nation uniform woodland|arctic|blackops`); issued gear is never dropped.
Right-click a soldier with any armor piece to hand it over; it replaces the issued piece in that slot.
Vehicles also come in all three paints: right-click one with green, white or black dye to repaint it.

### Paint your own flag

The nation leader sneak + right-clicks a Command Flag (or runs `/nation flag`) to open the flag painter: a 16x10
grid, the 16 dye colours, fill, mirror, undo and starter layouts. The design flies on every Command Flag of the
nation. **Blank Patches** (leather + string) used in the air are stamped with your flag; use the **Nation Patch**
while wearing chest armor to sew it onto your left shoulder. Soldiers wear their nation's patch automatically.

## Weapon wheel

Hold **G** to open the wheel: eight slots for guns, separate from your inventory. Point at a slot and release to draw
that gun (the one in your hand goes into its slot); click the centre to stash the gun you hold; right-click a slot
to drop it into your inventory; 1-8 selects directly. The wheel is saved with the player.

## Vehicles

| Vehicle | Seats | Primary (LMB) | Secondary (RMB) | Controls |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jeep | 3 | ring-mounted MG | — | W/S drive, A/D steer |
| Tank | 1 | explosive shell (turret follows your look) | coaxial MG | W/S drive, A/D pivot |
| Fighter Plane | 1 | twin wing guns | bombs | W/S throttle, mouse steers once airborne |
| Attack Helicopter | 2 | chin gun | rockets | Space climb, look down to descend, WASD move, mouse turns |

Right-click a block with the vehicle item to unload it, right-click to board, sneak to get out, sneak + empty hand to
pack it up. Vehicles have health; bullets and explosions wear them down and they blow up at zero. The camera switches
to third person while riding. Models are generated by `gen/gen_vehicles.py` (parts, textures and the preview).

## Nations and armies

1. Craft a **Command Flag** (red wool, gold nugget, sticks) and place it. The first flag opens the naming screen:
   nation name, army name, banner colour. Each flag claims 48 blocks around it (config); more flags are outposts.
2. Craft **Enlistment Papers** (paper, paper, ink sac, emerald) and right-click villagers to recruit them. Soldiers
   get a surname, a uniform in your colour, and rank up with kills (Pvt. → Cpl. → Sgt. → Lt.).
3. Right-click a soldier with a gun to arm them. Empty hand cycles their order: **Follow**, **Hold position**,
   **Patrol the base**. Sneak + right-click discharges them (they become a villager again).
4. The **Command Baton**: right-click to have nearby troops follow you, sneak + right-click to hold, right-click a
   block to rally them there.
5. `/nation info|list|invite <player>|join <name>|leave|kick|rename <name>|army <name>|color <name|hex>|war <name>|peace <name>|recall|disband`.
   Soldiers fight hostile mobs always, and enemy nations' soldiers and players only while at war.

## Building

CI (`.github/workflows/build.yml`) builds the jar to the `jar` branch and runs client/server smoke tests to the
`smoke-log` branch. Locally: `./gradlew build`.

`preview/index.html` is a three.js page with every gun, the first-person view (same maths as `FirstPersonGun`),
the soldier model and the flag. Regenerate with `python3 gen/gen_guns.py && python3 gen/gen_assets.py &&
python3 gen/gen_sounds.py && python3 gen/build_preview.py`.
